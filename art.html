Every piece here is a single original, painted by hand and signed. Prices are in US dollars and do not include shipping or framing; write and we will work out the best way to get it to you.

Buying

How it works

Write using the button on the piece you want. You will get an answer from Juan himself, not a form. He will confirm the work is still available, quote shipping to your city, and explain how the augmented layer works once the canvas is on your wall.

Works ship worldwide from Bogotá. Larger canvases usually travel rolled in a tube and are stretched on arrival, which is standard practice and keeps shipping sane.
